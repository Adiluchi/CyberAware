ABOUT OUR PLATFORM

Why CyberAware?

A suspicious email, unsafe link, or exposed password can put personal information at risk. CyberAware helps people understand common online threats and learn practical ways to protect themselves.

Explore short lessons, test your knowledge, check sample passwords against known breach data, and analyze synthetic emails for warning signs. Our goal is to make cybersecurity education accessible to everyone.
